## Correction: MTP ACKs were attributed to ANS

The earlier version misidentified `rtkit-helper 394600000.mtp` as an ANS
helper. It is the MTP keyboard/trackpad coprocessor. Its successful IOP/AP
ACKs do not describe the storage coprocessor. The claims that the ANS boot
wall moved and that C8 supports tracing-induced timing sensitivity are
retracted. This corrects the interpretation, not the stored evidence.

In cold run 2, kmsg [234]–[243] belongs to `rtkit-helper 394600000.mtp`;
the ANS reset and exchange belongs to `nvme-apple 4c5cc0000.nvme`.
Verify the device prefix on every message. The full handoff at
`/home/btega/Downloads/astra-handoff-c9-2026-10-12.md` records the correction.
Local OCR pages 14–15 and 16–20 retain these distinct device prefixes;
OCR is a locator, not verified hexadecimal transcription.
